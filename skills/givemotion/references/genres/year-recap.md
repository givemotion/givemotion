# Genre: year recap / stats recap ("wrapped", 15–30 s)

Purpose: structure, pacing and the moves that scored well for celebratory stat recaps.

| Time (18 s video) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Opener: the year in huge type, rolling or assembling, shapes already drifting |
| 2–15 s | One stat per moment, ~2.5 s each, cut on the beat: the number counts up big (160–260 px), with its own simple illustration that moves (medals dropping in, a route line drawing, a dot grid filling from 96 to 214, a pin dropping on a city) |
| 15–18 s | Finale: the year rolls over (2026 → 2027), confetti, the closing line landing late so the end keeps moving |

Rules:
- **Vary the layout per stat.** The weakest recap used "number top-left, graphic right" in every scene and read as animated slides. Alternate: number centered with the graphic around it, a full-frame illustration with the number inside it, a camera move along a route, a split screen.
- Tell a small story inside a stat: "214 members, up from 96" counts from 96 to 214 while the grid fills; do not sit at 96 for a second before counting.
- Flowing transitions with direction (`push-*`, `cover-*`, `iris` from the next stat's shape, `morph` of a shape into the next illustration), cut on the beat (`data-cuts="beats: …"`).
- Ambient shapes drifting behind every scene keep frames alive; keep them, and confetti, off the numbers (judges marked route lines and confetti crossing key numbers).
- Fill the frame: big empty areas next to a small number were marked down; scale the illustration up.
- End on motion: the final line ≤ 2 s of hold; a 3.5 s still ending was the worst-rated part of one recap.
- Bold Pop look (`references/styles.md`); colored type on colored blocks needs contrast (cream on orange failed).
