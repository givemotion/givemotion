### What the judges named (keyword counts over the 36 notes, so rough)

1. **Near-still holds** (24 notes mention still/static/frozen/hold), mostly the end card, usually 1.5-2.5 s.
2. **Small secondary text** (23): taglines, card subtitles, axis labels, code in an editor, labels in the final wide shot.
3. **Text cut by the frame edge** (17), during or after camera moves; often a neighbouring part's label half in
   frame while the shot is about something else.
4. **Empty space** (15): layouts crowding one corner or the top half, nearly empty frames mid-transition.
5. **Ghosted or overlapping text** (8), mostly in crossfades; a nearly empty first frame (4).

## Friction logs

56 engine, 48 docs, 31 agent entries. The engine items that came up more than once, and what changed (all on `main`):

| Friction (runs) | Fix |
|---|---|
| code-window `⟦ ⟧` marks shifted one character per line and split lines (2) | Offsets no longer count the newlines that the per-line spans drop; test |
| `zoom-through` showed a small see-through box mid-transition (2) | The old scene flies past on top; the new one waits full size behind it |
| `text-over-line` on labels with an opaque panel or backing over the line (4) | A stroke hidden under something opaque at that point does not count; dashed lines now count (a dashed ring through a label was missed); denser sampling |
| `low-contrast` error on white SVG text (2) | SVG text is judged by its `fill`, and is hidden for the background capture |
| `low-contrast` during fades, under a scrim, or when a caption half covers a label (2) | Text that is still fading or lies under another element is not judged for contrast |
| A tween showed its end state on the exact frame it starts (1, but every "round" start time could hit it) | Float error put the frame a hair before the start (cycle -1); local time is clamped |
| `gm.burst`: wrong place in a camera world, `behind` ignored there, sparks too small, units undocumented (4) | The canvas lives in the right layer (before `behind`, or in the emitter's camera world), follows the camera, is sized to the particles; bigger sparks; `'960px'` strings; defaults documented |
| `fromTo` with `keyframes` ignored its from-values and `hideBefore` (1) | Applied to the first step |
| Camera `fit` on an element scaled to 0 before it pops in zoomed in on a dot (1) | Such an element is framed at its full size |
| A camera shot that arrives after the scene starts to leave is never seen (2) | `camera-late` warning |
| Beat pulse needs hand-written `onFrame` glue (3); `data-cuts` cannot be written in beats (1) | `data-pulse="beat 0.06"` (scale and a `--beat` variable); `data-cuts="beats: 0 4 8"` / `"bars: …"` |
| Intentional wide shots of a whole UI raise 15 `tiny-text` warnings (3) | Shot option `overview: true` |
| Nearly empty opening not flagged (2) | `empty-start` measures edges, so a gradient-only opening counts as empty |
| White fill under `data-sketch` hatched pink (1) | Hue-less fills hatch in grey |
| Captions block kept its last line 0.8 s into the next scene (2) | `hold` param |
| Where a scene's time 0 is with `data-cut-align="middle"` (3); whether `music.*` take video or clip time (2); `data-enter` replacing the CSS `translate` (1) | Documented |

From the judges' list: half-cut labels are reported again even when the shot is about something else (an earlier
change in this round had silenced them because makers found them noisy; the judges show they matter), `still`
fires from 1.9 s at the very end, `tiny-text` from 22 px (was 18), new `ghost-overlap` warning, and the layout
section asks for ≥ 32 px secondary text on screen.

Not reproduced: the year-recap maker saw a scene lose its background after an extra top-level `const` line; the
final file renders correctly with and without that line on both engines.
