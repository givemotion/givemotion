# Brief: "Lumen" desk lamp product film

A 16-second product film for Lumen, a desk lamp. 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 16.0 s. No audio.

Palette: graphite #1E1E1E (background), paper #FAFAF7 (text), warm #FFC861 (accent: the lamp's light), steel #8A8F98 (muted).
Fonts: Space Grotesk for headlines, Inter for labels.

Draw the lamp yourself (simple flat shapes: base, arm, head). Four scenes of about 4 s each, joined by transitions of your choice:
1. Dark frame; the lamp switches on and a warm cone of light fills the frame. Title "Lumen" appears in the light.
2. "Bends where you need it." The lamp's arm rotates through two positions; the light cone follows.
3. "From 2700K to 6500K." The light color slides from warm to cool and back; a small slider shows the value.
4. "Lumen. Light that stays out of the way." with "lumen.design" small below. Hold at least 1.5 s.

The four scenes must look like one film (same lamp, same light, same type). No other copy.
