# Brief: "Tidepool" app launch teaser

A 12-second teaser for Tidepool, a calm budgeting app. 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 12.0 s. No audio.

Palette: deep sea #0E2A3A (background), foam #F2F5F0 (text), coral #FF7A59 (accent, use sparingly), kelp #3FA796 (secondary).
Fonts: Space Grotesk for headlines, Inter for everything else.

Beats:
1. 0-4 s: The headline "Money, without the waves." builds word by word. "waves" is the key word. A slow wave line drifts behind it.
2. 4-8 s: The wave line becomes the edge of a phone-sized card showing "This month" and a balance that counts from $0 to $2,480. Three small category chips (Rent, Food, Fun) land under it, one after another.
3. 8-12 s: The card shrinks into the Tidepool logo (a simple circle with a wave inside, drawn by you) and the wordmark "Tidepool" appears beside it, with "Out on iOS, May 4" below. Hold the end card at least 2 s.

The three beats should flow as one piece (one thing becomes the next), not cut like slides. No other copy.
