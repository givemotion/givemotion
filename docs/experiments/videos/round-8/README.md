# Round 8 videos (2026-11-06)

Every video made in round 8 of the random-brief test, for all three tools, unedited. Each
brief was made by two independent makers per tool (sets 1 and 2, e.g. K and K2). Click a video to play it.

Versions: givemotion 0.1.4 (development branch), Remotion 4.0.541, HyperFrames 0.9.18; makers Claude Opus 5.5 at
effort high, every tool open to all makers; six blind judges per set. Scores are the judges' mean overall (1-10);
the best in each set is in bold. Method, judge notes and caveats:
[the rounds 7-8 report](../../2026-11-rounds-7-8.md) and [all rounds](../../2026-11-random-briefs.md).

**About these files:** to keep the repository small they are re-encoded once, with the same settings for every
tool (H.264, CRF 22, slow preset), from the makers' own renders. The difference from the originals is not visible
(52-54 dB PSNR on a sample); the originals are 1.5-3x larger. No audio (the briefs had none).
