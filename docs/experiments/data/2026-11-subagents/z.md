# Brief: "Four habits for better sleep"

A 16-second explainer for a health newsletter. 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 16.0 s. No audio.

Palette: night #1B1F3B (background), moon #F5F1E6 (text), lilac #A99BFF (accent), dusk #4A4F7A (muted).
Fonts: Nunito for everything (bold for headlines).

Four scenes of about 4 s each, joined by transitions of your choice. Each scene has a simple icon you draw yourself (SVG or CSS shapes), a number, and one line:
1. "01 Same bedtime, every night." Icon: a clock whose hands sweep to 10:30.
2. "02 Screens off an hour before." Icon: a phone whose screen dims to dark.
3. "03 Keep it cool: 18 °C." Icon: a thermometer whose level drops. The "18 °C" is the key word and counts down from 24.
4. "04 Morning light, first thing." Icon: a sun rising over a line. Then the newsletter name "Rested" appears small under it. Hold the last frame at least 1.5 s.

In each line one key word gets the accent. The four scenes must look like one video (same layout system, sizes and motion). No other copy.
