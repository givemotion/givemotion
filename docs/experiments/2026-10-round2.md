# Round 2: did the engine work since round 1 make better videos?

October 2026. Same method as [round 1](2026-10-format-test.md): a fresh Claude Opus agent gets only the docs
and one brief, makes a video, and blind Opus judges score 2 fps contact sheets (1-10).

## Repeated briefs (round 1 vs round 2)

Each judge saw three versions of the same brief in random order: round 1 HTML, round 1 React, round 2.

| Judge | R1 HTML | R1 React | R2 | R2 rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| promo 1 | 7 | 8 | 7 | 3rd |
| promo 2 | 7 | 9 | 7.5 | 2nd |
| explainer 1 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 3rd |
| explainer 2 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 3rd |

Round 2 did **not** beat round 1 (R2 average 6.6, round 1 average 7.6). With one run per brief, the
difference between runs of the same model is bigger than the effect of the engine changes. The judges'
reasons for low scores were concrete, and most of them were things `check` did not catch.

## New briefs (round 2 only)

| Brief | Overall | Main problems the judges named |
|---|---|---|
| UI demo | 7 | Zoomed shots cut cards and the date line at the frame edge; ~5 s of near-still holds |
| Data story | 7 | Highlight labels cut at the left edge while the camera pans; counter out of sync with the line; long still end |
| Logo sting | 7 | Second half has little energy; wordmark overlaps the badge for a moment |
| Year recap | 8 | Route line and confetti pass over key numbers; rushed ending |

## What changed in the engine because of this

1. **Labels cut by the frame in camera shots.** `check` treated everything inside a camera world as
   "framing" and said nothing. Now a label that stays mostly in view but cut for 0.3 s or more is a warning.
2. **See-through layers hid text from checks.** A full-frame chart `<svg>` (or particle `<canvas>`, or a
   gradient vignette) on top of the text made `check` think the text was covered, so it skipped it.
   Now only things that paint a solid surface count as covering.
3. **Motion measured against the camera.** Text that sits still in a world the camera pans over is not
   "moving" any more, so it gets checked.
4. **Pacing.** New `still` warning: 2.5 s or more where nothing enters, leaves or moves and under 5% of
   the picture changes. The most common judge complaint in both rounds was holds that feel stalled.

Re-running `check` on the round-2 videos now reports the cut labels the judges saw in the data story and
the UI demo, and the still ending of the UI demo.

## Not fixed (author logic or judge noise)

- Counter out of sync with the chart: the agent drove them with two separate timings.
- The sting tagline judged "barely readable" is fine at full size; the judges saw small sheets.

## Render speed note

On the 2 vCPU test machine with no GPU, agent-made pages render at 2.5-3 fps per worker, against
18 fps for the benchmark page. A trace shows the time is spread over software raster and compositing of
many full-frame layers (backgrounds, a camera world that changes scale every frame, grain, glow). No
single element or Chrome flag made a big difference. Machines with a GPU are much faster.
