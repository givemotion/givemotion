# Made by AI agents, from the docs alone

Two fresh AI agents (that had never seen givemotion) were given only `skills/givemotion/SKILL.md`, the block files and the examples, plus a one-paragraph brief. They wrote these compositions, used `check` and `snapshot` to fix their own mistakes, and rendered the videos. Their feedback was used to improve the tool and the docs.

- `photosynthesis/` — "How plants make food", a 30 s kids explainer with hand-built SVG illustrations.
- `coffee-promo/` — a 15 s vertical promo with kinetic type, a price counter, captions, SVG steam drawn with `gm.onFrame`, a music bed, plus a transparent logo overlay (`overlay.html`, render with `--transparent -o overlay.mov`).
